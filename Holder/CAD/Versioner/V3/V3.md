The third version is also a "sandwich"-design (further development of V1 and V2) in which the holder consists of a top and bottom with the battery in between.

* Forces in X and Y are conastrined by integrated supports in the holder.
* The model does take the swelling in the Z-direction in to consideration with built in springs.
* As much material as possible was removed in order to not disturb the imaging process.
* No support for the sensors were created.
* The measurements are correct.

The problem with V2 was that once the cell was centered in the scan and rotated, the cell was placed out of centre since it was not aligned with the axis of rotation. This was fixed for V3. No other changes were made.
