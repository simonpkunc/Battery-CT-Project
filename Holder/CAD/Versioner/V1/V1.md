The first version is a "sandwich"-design in which the holder consists of a top and bottom with the battery in between.

* Forces in X and Y are conastrined by integrated supports in the holder.
* The model does not take the swelling in the Z-direction in to consideration which is yet to be determined.
* Only the bottom part was 3D-printed in order to show Marios and Artem for feedback.
* As much material as possible was removed in order to not disturb the imaging process.
* No support for the sensors were created.
* The measurements are not correct, they're estimated.

This model was only created as a prototype to see if the direction of the design was right or not.
